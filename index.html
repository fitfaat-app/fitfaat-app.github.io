FitFaat: Gym Management Software & App for Gyms in India

FitFaat is gym management software and a gym management app that runs a whole fitness business from one platform. Since 2019 it has helped more than 1,000 gyms, sports academies, yoga studios and fitness chains across India replace registers and spreadsheets with automation.

Visit fitfaat.com Gym Management Application (Android) Gym Management App (iOS)

What FitFaat does

  • Membership billing: plans, renewals, upgrades and freezes, GST invoices, due tracking, and online renewals through UPI, cards and net banking.
  • Attendance and access control: fingerprint, face recognition, RFID and QR check-ins with eSSL, ZKTeco, Biomax and Timmy devices; access is blocked automatically for expired members.
  • Gym CRM and lead management: capture enquiries from your website, social media, calls and walk-ins, and track follow-ups, trials and conversions.
  • WhatsApp automation: renewal reminders, payment alerts and birthday wishes by WhatsApp, SMS, email and push notification.
  • Multi-branch management: run several locations from one dashboard, with separate roles for trainers, reception, sales and admin.

Apps for owners, trainers and members

The owner app, FitFaat Partner, puts collections, attendance, expiries and reports in your pocket on Android and iOS. Members get a branded app to book slots, renew online and follow workout and diet plans.
